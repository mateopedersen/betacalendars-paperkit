# ``BetaCalendarsPaperKit``

Build calendar grids and printable sheets from timezone-independent Gregorian civil dates.

PaperKit separates a civil month, its seven-column grid topology, paper geometry, and rendering. The same computed model powers SwiftUI previews and vector PDF output. Blank planner sheets share the paper system without inventing dates.
